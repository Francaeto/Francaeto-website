Refund policy

When you can cancel, and how refunds work for counselling sessions and app purchases.

Request a consultation

This policy applies to payments made to Francaeto Counselling & Consulting Services (Franca Eto Umukoro), whether by card through Stripe or in Naira through Flutterwave. It does not affect your legal rights as a consumer.

Free consultation

The first 15-minute consultation is free, so there is nothing to refund.

Counselling sessions

  • Cancelling with at least 24 hours’ notice on a working day (Monday to Friday): you won’t be charged. If you have already paid for that session, I will refund it in full or move it to another date, whichever you prefer.
  • Cancelling with less notice than this, or not attending: the full session fee is charged and is not refundable.
  • Illness: if you are unwell and give at least 24 hours’ notice on a working day, you won’t be charged.
  • If I cancel: if I need to cancel a session, I will offer another time or refund that session in full.
  • Sessions paid in advance: if you paid for a block of sessions and decide to stop, I will refund unused sessions that you cancel in line with this policy (at least 24 hours’ notice on a working day). Unused sessions cancelled with less notice, or missed, are not refunded. Where you received a discount for paying in advance, the refund is worked out at the full single-session price for the sessions already used.

Wellbeing apps (Tend, Break the Cycle and Anchor)

  • Monthly and yearly card subscriptions: you can cancel at any time and you won’t be charged again. Premium stays open until the end of the period you have already paid for. Part-used periods are not refunded.
  • Naira payments: these are one-off payments for one month or one year, and they don’t renew automatically.
  • 14-day right to cancel: you can cancel within 14 days of buying and get a full refund, as long as Premium has not yet been unlocked. Premium is digital content, so once it has been unlocked on your device the 14-day right to cancel ends, as explained before you pay.
  • Problems with a payment: if you are charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or Premium doesn’t unlock and we can’t fix it, you will receive a full refund.

Books

My books are sold by Amazon, so Amazon’s own returns and refund policy applies.

How to ask for a refund

Email info@francaeto.com with your name, the date, the amount and how you paid (card or Naira). I aim to reply within 2 working days.

Approved refunds are paid back to the card or account you paid with, through the same payment provider (Stripe or Flutterwave). Refunds are usually processed within 5 working days, and your bank may take a few more days to show them.

Questions or complaints

If you’re unhappy with a decision about a refund, please email me and I will look at it again. This policy doesn’t affect your statutory rights.
