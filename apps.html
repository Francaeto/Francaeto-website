From the creator

Why I created these apps

In my work as a therapist, I see how much difference the right support makes in the moments between sessions.

I created Tend, Break the Cycle and Anchor so that practical, evidence-informed tools are there whenever you need them: to calm your mind, to understand and change unhelpful patterns, or to support someone you love while looking after yourself.
